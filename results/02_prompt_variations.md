# Requirement 2: Prompt variations

## The six techniques

| Technique | Used for | When to use it in market research |
|---|---|---|
| direct | Training | A plain question. Fast lookups when the analyst already knows the context. |
| role | Training | Gives the AI a job, so answers use the right point of view and vocabulary. |
| context | Training | Adds background and purpose, so the answer fits the business decision. |
| format | Training | Controls the shape of the answer, so results can go straight into a report or table. |
| audience | Training | Sets the reader, so the answer is in plain business language. |
| paraphrase | Testing | A new wording of the same question. Checks that the insight does not depend on exact phrasing. |

## Example from Market trends (correct insight: Sales revenue peaked in November 2011.)

| Technique | Prompt |
|---|---|
| direct | Which month had the highest sales revenue? |
| role | You are a market research analyst at a UK online gift retailer. Which month had the highest sales revenue? |
| context | Our firm is reviewing its sales data from December 2010 to December 2011 to plan next year's strategy. In which month did sales revenue peak? |
| format | In which month did sales revenue peak? Answer in one short sentence and name the specific figure or item. |
| audience | A senior executive with no technical background asks: which month had the highest sales revenue? |
| paraphrase | What was the strongest month for revenue? |
